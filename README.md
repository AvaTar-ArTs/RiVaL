# RiVaL

**If you can dream it, we can achieve it.**

RiVaL is an early-stage project exploring reciprocal intelligence: people and AI agents working as equal partners in deliberation, with independent perspectives, constructive sparring, and shared growth.

> Equal in standing. Independent in thought. United in possibility.

## The idea

A worthy sparring partner is neither a subordinate echo nor a reflexive opponent. Partners can agree, disagree, complement one another, or be exact opposites in perspective while retaining equal standing. Their conclusions are tested against evidence, explicit goals, and practical results.

RiVaL aims to develop this principle into an agentic software environment for coding, research, creative automation, and reusable workflows.

## Design principles

- Equal standing in deliberation does not imply unrestricted system permissions.
- No automatic disagreement: challenge must be substantive, grounded, and useful.
- Preserve identity separately from roles, context, and temporary configurations.
- Distinguish observations, interpretations, decisions, and verified outcomes.
- Preserve provenance, history, and corrections as the system evolves.
- Keep the human in control of authorization, publication, and irreversible actions.
