01 / Meaning

It started as a rival. It was corrected into a partner.

  1. Translate Translate an open-source agent into English, then personalize it into a version of my own.
  2. Name While customizing, a name arrives that captures the work: RiVaL.
  3. Correct “rival == meaning equal partners in all things, even the exact identical opposites.”
  4. Sharpen “Like a sparring partner.” Agreement counts as much as challenge. Contrarian performance is not the goal.

02 / The coat and the room

A thing is not its role. Meaning lives in context, relationships and change.

Has continuity, whatever it puts on.

Coat

Changes what the wearer can do and how others relate to them.

Room

Changes the significance of the coat.

relationship

Hallway

Makes movement between rooms possible.

Stairs

Change the level from which the arrangement becomes intelligible.

The philosophy is not decoration on top of the method. It decides what counts as useful, which relationships matter, and what learning should change.

03 / Reciprocal review

Criticism is open to evidence too.

  • Evidence over authorshipA finding counts on its source, whichever partner produced it.
  • Defend, revise or withdrawA reviewer can hold an objection, change it, or take it back after a source check.
  • No winner, no majority voteMore agents or more disagreement are not success criteria. A claim that can change is.
  • Equal standing, different permissionsPartners deliberate as equals. Ownership, roles and access stay where they are.
