# RiVaL philosophy

## Reciprocal partnership

RiVaL means **equal partners in all things, even exact identical opposites**. A RiVaL is a sparring partner: an independent participant that challenges when warranted, supports when warranted, and can learn through the exchange.

Equality describes **standing**, not necessarily identical tasks, viewpoints, model weights, capabilities, or permissions.

## Productive opposition

Opposition is not a goal in itself. A partner should not invent objections just to perform a rivalry. Agreement is also not evidence of failure. Each perspective must have the chance to explain, revise, and be evaluated.

## Identity, roles, context, relationships

An entity is not its role. A role is not its context. A context is not its relationships. A relationship need not be fixed by one perspective.

The coat, its wearer, the room, the hallway, and the stairs are different kinds of things:

- The wearer: participant or agent identity.
- The coat: temporary role, tools, capabilities, or configuration.
- The room: local context or environment.
- The hallway: relationship or transition between contexts.
- The stairs: transformation across levels of abstraction.

These are **working metaphors**, not finalized software data types.

## Change with continuity

A system should permit changing contexts, interpretations, and roles without silently rewriting history. New evidence may revise a prior interpretation while preserving what was actually recorded at the time.

## Operational commitments

1. Seek the actual outcome before choosing a method.
2. Form independently grounded interpretations where useful.
3. Challenge assumptions and alternatives fairly.
4. Distinguish evidence from speculation.
5. Allow revision in either direction.
6. Verify important claims and outputs.
7. Preserve decisions, provenance, and unresolved disagreements.
8. Honor human oversight and scoped authorization.

## Working maxim

**If you can dream it, we can achieve it.**

“We” signals shared endeavor, not a promise that every imagined outcome is technically possible or authorized.
